13 October 2026 — MarbleStudio preview performance

Scope: all 11 modes reviewed. Existing unrelated working-tree edits preserved.

Changes
- src/preview-canvas.js: shared display/DPR sizing, minimum 540 portrait or 960
  landscape pixels wide; resizing is applied with the next draw, not mid-capture.
- app.js, renderer.js, territory-renderer.js, pi-studio.js, statistics.js:
  adaptive previews for track, arena, territory, spiral, Pi and statistics.
  At 1366x1000 most portrait previews are 540x960 instead of 1080x1920
  (75% fewer pixels). Pi uses 576x1024; statistics uses 960x540.
- Portrait recording stays 1080x1920; statistics capture/export stays 1920x1080.
  Statistics explicitly captures at 60 fps and restores preview after stopping
  or a start failure. Offline renderFrame explicitly requests full resolution.
- canvas-layer.js: one cached static layer per output context, invalidated by
  theme/key or size. Ring background, glass background and spiral-breaker
  mascots use this cache. Existing 540x960 native previews retain their size.
- Ball duel batches background dots into one fill; RPS caches title fitting;
  Pi throttles DOM updates; ring counters update only when their text changes.
- Earlier arena swept-cut fixes are retained and regression-tested.
