2026-10-15: Ranged troops exchange ranks with friendly melee.

Implementation
- src/crown-battle-screening.js: foot ranged troops detect local melee pressure,
  find a living friendly melee screen (including another allied army), and move
  behind it with a lateral offset. An urgent retreat cancels unreleased shots.
- Melee walking towards enemies requests a brief sideways passage from ranged
  blockers; ordinary shots finish first. Lateral deployment does not request it.
- Explicit hold/move/attack orders retain priority. Mounted archers are excluded
  and belong to the other thread's mounted-skirmish work.
- crown-battle.js integrates screening before autonomous tactics and passage
  requests into existing bounded walk avoidance. No whole-army scan added.
- historical-civilization-tactics.js counts active rank exchange as responding
  during assembly; otherwise Ottoman assembly timed out against advancing foes.
- Threat/support queries run every 0.35-0.45 seconds with 24/40 candidate caps.
  Threat radius 100, support radius 125; retreat attempts bounded to 7 seconds.
  This is local avoidance, not a guarantee that every surrounded unit escapes.
