Follow-up: battle-start stalls (2026-10-15)
The earlier 'final' measurements above are superseded by this follow-up.
- Fixed zoom-independent atlas density (1.5 for armies >=160); 12-type fixture fits 740 sources in 13 pages without cycling through evictions. Historical horse variants 0/2 share their identical pose.
- Tile readback and premultiplication happen within the pose bake budget. GPU uploads reuse the bounded page pixel buffer; no repeated dirty-region canvas reads during drawing.
- Maximum one new runtime pose per frame when a prior pose is available; idle preparation also includes initial idle and hussar charge poses.
- Audio DSP runs in a local module worker; fallback sounds are prepared before simulation and never synthesized in play().
- Start waits for pose/audio preparation, shows progress, supports cancel, and invalidates on rebuild/hidden tab. Cold fixture preparation measured 8-10 seconds before simulation starts.
- Large army canvas DPR capped at 1. This trades fine zoom detail for lower drawing cost.

Follow-up: higher army capacity (2026-10-15)
- Runtime units now share immutable combat metadata (config, role, ranged flag, radius) through a uniform object-literal field. Plain preview/test units retain the type lookup fallback. staggerX is initialized rather than changing the unit shape during a charge hit.
- Renderer caches sprite families and animation keys instead of creating identity/frame strings for every soldier on every draw. WeakMap unit records validate type/team/id; facing selects the existing key set.
- Coordinated with ME art thread: sourceResolution includes visualScale, up to native density / 6x. Ordinary large-army density stays 1.5; giant poses retain detail. Both warmup and live cache use the same density.
- Rejected and reverted scratch neighbor arrays, defineProperty metadata, and a nearLocal loop rearrangement: they did not improve representative dense workloads. Spatial ordering and battle behavior remain unchanged.
- Benchmark now records actual render intervals and simulation/wall time, not only RAF callbacks; optional engine/renderer snapshots permit identical current dependencies. Missing sprites / WebGL errors fail the probe; atlas revision delta exposes eviction churn.
