Changes in this continuation:
- LocalBattleGrid: 16-unit cells for walking/body separation, bounded dense
  sampling. Existing 40-unit grid remains authoritative for swept projectiles
  and cavalry contacts. Smaller cells reduce irrelevant neighbour work.
- Large crowds use dedicated allied ranged grids for lane warnings, avoiding
  repeated scans through melee ranks. Small battles retain previous lookup.
- At 6000+ total soldiers and 4x, request 10 simulation updates per playback
  second (400ms simulated per tick). Other crowds retain 20 updates/second.
  GPU interpolation continues between completed ticks. Keep the 9ms frame
  work budget, pending-tick completion and bounded catch-up.
- Carry a coarse tick's cooldown overrun into the next attack so sustained
  weapon cadence does not round up by 400ms on every swing. No idle banking.
- Synchronous replay/export retains its previous step limit. Coarse live
  stepping trades temporal precision for responsiveness; battle outcomes
  are not guaranteed identical to fixed 50ms simulation.
