Implementation:
- src/crown-battle-movement-sound.js samples at most 96 array positions plus two
  previous representatives every 0.12 seconds, selecting nearby moving infantry
  and horse cavalry. Mounted archers are included. Elephants, giants and known
  non-horse fantasy mounts are excluded pending matching recordings.
- src/crown-battle-feedback.js integrates movement sampling with the listener.
- src/crown-battle-audio.js decodes movement once, shares the existing worker,
  limits movement to two voices within the eight total, reserves foreground
  capacity and changes playback pace with movement speed. Pause/mute stop it.
  Missing movement recordings remain silent. No engine RNG/state changes.
- A shared 2200 Hz low-pass bus muffles distant movement and weapon contacts;
  camera-relative gain/pan remain active. No additional per-hit filters.
- src/crown-battle-sound-design.js caps prepared feet at 0.32s and hoof at 0.6s.
