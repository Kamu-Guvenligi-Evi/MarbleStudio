Taç Savaşı — crowded battle throughput follow-up, 2026-10-16

User reports continuing severe slowdown at 3,000–10,000 soldiers.
The previous 6 ms sliced loop kept controls responsive but discarded elapsed
time and left too little CPU time to complete combat ticks.

Changes:
- src/crown-battle-timing.js and crown-battle-app.js: bounded catch-up queue,
  multiple completed ticks per frame when affordable, 20 Hz combat at >=2,500
  soldiers (engine's supported 0.05 s maximum); 18 ms crowd work allowance.
  Smaller battles retain 30 Hz. All soldiers still update. Pending ticks resume
  without restarting; normal 1x/2x/4x rates work when CPU capacity permits.
- crown-battle.js: move unit update/separation bodies out of generators into
  ordinary methods. Preserve loop order, random calls and 32-unit yields.
- crown-battle-renderer.js: adapt crowd redraw cadence to measured drawing
  cost, maximum 30 Hz, interval capped at 150 ms. Slow draws leave CPU time for
  combat. Camera, selection, resize and pause invalidations draw immediately.
  Soldier count, sprite resolution, poses and collision checks are retained.

Validation passed:
- crown-battle-sliced-simulation.mjs with start-of-turn engine snapshot:
  exact state hashes at 15/30/45 ticks for 320/3,000/10,000 units, including
  movement, damage, projectiles and results. This verifies the engine refactor
  at the same timestep; changing live pacing to 20 Hz need not produce identical
  outcomes to a 30 Hz battle.
- crown-battle-timing.mjs: counts 320/3,000/10,000, rates 1x/2x/4x, partial ticks,
  bounded backlog/work and finished battles.
- crown-battle-render-pacing.mjs: costly drawing, bounded redraw delay,
  camera/selection/resize/pause invalidation, unchanged paused scene, small army.
- crown-battle-physical-contact.mjs and crown-battle-projectiles.mjs:
  physical contacts at 20/30/60/120 Hz, swept projectile hits and charge rules.
- crown-battle-contact-query.mjs: 3,000 rays against 2,400 bodies, exhaustive oracle.
- crown-battle-crowd-controls-browser.mjs: actual app at 10,000 soldiers,
  zoom, pause mid-tick, resume, restart; no page errors.
- Vite production build exit 0; existing >500 kB chunk-size warning remains.

Local Chrome benchmark, mixed armies deliberately packed into contact:
tests/crown-battle-throughput-browser.mjs, 5 seconds warm-up then ~13 seconds
measurement; completed simulation ticks counted (not merely pending tick time).
Final 10,000 paired run:
  Before: 13.0408 wall seconds, 1.1000 completed simulation seconds (0.0844x).
  After:  12.9952 wall seconds, 12.3000 completed simulation seconds (0.9465x).
Final 3,000 current run:
  13.0410 wall seconds, 12.3500 completed simulation seconds (0.9470x).
All soldiers retained, no page/WebGL errors.

Limits:
Other local CPU-heavy tasks ran during this session. Earlier current-build
10,000 runs achieved only 0.186x; measurements are load-sensitive, not a
controlled universal speedup claim. Different progress also means different
combat phases. Near-real-time simulation is not smooth visible rendering:
the final 10,000 run had 55.6 ms p95 RAF intervals and 61.1 ms maximum drawing
cost; skipped draws count as zero in draw statistics. Adaptive redraw trades
animation frequency for simulation progress. No 60 FPS guarantee. General
grid/tactics work and a single unit group can still exceed the soft time budget.
CPU profiles identify local neighbor queries and sprite drawing as remaining
costs. Several packed-position/finer-grid experiments were reverted because
they did not reliably help; the existing spatial sampling remains unchanged.
