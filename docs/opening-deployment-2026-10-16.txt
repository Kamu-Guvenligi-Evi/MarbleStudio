Automatic commander opening deployment — 2026-10-16

Request: automatic armies should spawn in opponent-aware ranks instead of
spending the opening seconds assembling through friendly troops.

Implementation
- src/historical-opening-deployment.js runs the existing strategy, historical
  doctrine and generic formation planners before the first simulation tick.
  Actual tactical slots determine initial positions; no independent duplicate
  historical formation definitions were introduced.
- Historical army deployment first establishes local opposing fronts, then
  packs complete plans into separate allied areas. Fronts share a safe gap;
  Seljuk wings include their required maneuver envelope. Non-doctrine support
  troops receive separate files. Bounds grow with the resulting positions.
- HistoricalTactics adopts unchanged opening plans on the first live tick.
  Civilizations and generic formations preserve their adopted state. Seljuk
  bait still requires real enemy pursuit; readiness does not trigger a fake
  retreat automatically.
- Manual orders/positions, commander loss and roster changes invalidate cached
  orders. Disabled tactical AI and fortress maps bypass opening planning.
- Legacy historicalArmies constructors retain their prior behavior; the army
  configuration UI uses armyConfigs and receives the new opening deployment.
- Existing researched doctrine definitions remain game interpretations; this
  change connects those definitions to deployment, not a new historical claim.

Validation
- Added tests/historical-opening-deployment.mjs: five enemy responses mirrored
  across both teams; ten civilization doctrines; Seljuk support; chariots,
  elephants and horse archers; exact initial slots and first-order continuity;
  invalidation and empty armies; six allied armies with maneuver space.
- Passed historical-army-deployment (2/4/8 armies, up to 1,000 troops per army),
  historical-deployment-spacing, historical-commander-formations,
  historical-preparation-pressure, crown-battle-early-yield,
  historical-alliance-coordination, historical-multi-army-tactics,
  historical-adaptive-battles. Earlier in task: commander-deployment,
  commander-strategy and alliance-redeployment also passed.
- Existing stalled-assembly test now explicitly sets an assembly phase after
  verifying that an automatically deployed army immediately advances.
- Browser: 484 units, four armies, rendered opening, 30 simulated seconds,
  finite positions, 243 wounded. Screenshot inspected locally via small JPEG.
- Vite production build passed; existing large-chunk warning remains.
- During concurrent work a temporary catalogue kind mismatch interrupted two
  tests. It was resolved externally before rerunning; no catalogue edit was
  made for this task. Both tests passed on rerun.
