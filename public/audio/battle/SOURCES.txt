sword-clash-01.ogg through sword-clash-06.ogg:
StarNinjas, "20 Sword Sound Effects (attacks and clashes)".
https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes
Source: sword_clash_-_starninjas_0.zip, sword_clash.1.ogg through .6.ogg.
Original audio unchanged, filenames changed. Author describes recording two
knives against each other, edited in Audacity. These are Foley recordings,
not a historical battlefield recording.

bow-release.wav:
artisticdude, "Battle sound effects", submitted by Ogrebane.
https://opengameart.org/content/battle-sound-effects
Source: battle_sound_effects_0.zip / battle_sound_effects/Bow.wav.
Original file unchanged, filename changed. CC0 option selected from offered
licenses. Source describes a bow effect for SummoningWars; it does not
establish that this is a recording of a real bow.

black-powder-01.wav through black-powder-03.wav:
kurt, "Gunshots".
https://opengameart.org/node/70780
Source: Black Powder.wav, author describes actual gunshot recordings.
First three individual shots extracted, downmixed to mono, resampled to
24000 Hz, saved as 16-bit PCM WAV. No synthetic gun body added.

Kenney, Impact Sounds 1.0, Creative Commons Zero (CC0).
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original license included as KENNEY-LICENSE.txt.

Original OGG files, unchanged:
impactMetal_medium_000/001/002.ogg: legacy files, no longer selected
impactPunch_medium_000/001/002.ogg: dry body/projectile impact
impactWood_heavy_000/001/002.ogg: heavy contact

Runtime: mono, leading-silence trim, light filtering/DC removal, edge fades,
bounded attack loudness. Natural recorded decay retained; no synthesized
overlay on loaded clips. Pitch, level and contact spacing vary slightly.
Dense armored melee adds a stereo texture rendered once from these same
blade/body clips. It activates only from nearby clash events and fades
within 0.65 seconds after renewal stops. One voice, included in the total
eight-voice cap, stopped on pause/mute. No perpetual battlefield loop.
Camera-relative panning and distance gain apply to foreground contacts.
Procedural samples are used only if an entire weapon bank fails to load.
No external audio requests at runtime, no microphone access.

horse-grass.mp3 and horse-ground.mp3:
congusbongus, "Horse gallop on different surfaces".
https://opengameart.org/content/horse-gallop-on-different-surfaces
Source: archive_2.zip / grass.mp3 and ground.mp3, unchanged, renamed.
The pack has mixed licences; ONLY the following CC0 entries are used:
grass: derived from SRS_Foley_Horse_Galloping.wav by StephenSaldanha
https://freesound.org/people/StephenSaldanha/sounds/165532/
ground: derived from Single Horse Galopp by D4XX
https://freesound.org/people/D4XX/sounds/564628/
Both explicitly marked CC0 in the archive's credits.txt, retained in
artifacts/battle-audio-recordings/horse-credits.txt. No CC-BY entries used.
These are gallop effects/Foley; no claim that all are live horse recordings.

Movement uses at most two voices within the same eight-voice budget.
Only moving, living nearby units initiate short steps; horse archers included.
Giant creatures, elephants and non-horse fantasy mounts are excluded until
matching recordings exist. Missing movement clips remain silent.
Runtime movement trim caps: feet 0.32s, hoof cycles 0.6s; otherwise the same
edge fades and bounded loudness treatment as weapons. Distant contacts and
movement use a shared 2200 Hz low-pass bus, with camera-relative level/pan.

Contact additions, 2026-10-15:
impactMetal_light_000.ogg through _002.ogg and
impactSoft_medium_000.ogg through _002.ogg:
Kenney, Impact Sounds, CC0; unchanged originals from the already-local
artifacts/battle-audio/impact.zip. See KENNEY-LICENSE.txt.
https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
Generic Foley effects used to distinguish projectile hits on armor from soft
targets; these are not claimed to be actual arrow/armor recordings.
Prepared once: metal max 0.22s, soft max 0.25s. One voice per accepted contact,
sharing the existing hit cooldown and eight-voice limit. Missing variants fall
back to the existing hit bank. Projectile hits do not start the melee crowd bed.

Arrow revision, 2026-10-16:
Original assets unchanged. Bow.wav now uses a 0.16s dry release profile with
reduced low-frequency body, softened high frequencies and a damped tail.
Arrow/bolt contacts use six separately prepared variants derived from
impactSoft_heavy_000.ogg through _002.ogg: armor <=0.18s, soft <=0.20s.
These three unchanged originals are Kenney Impact Sounds, CC0, extracted
from the already-local artifacts/battle-audio/impact.zip; see KENNEY-LICENSE.txt.
They replace the earlier metal-light/soft-medium arrow profiles. A softened
4-5ms attack and damped high frequencies reduce the bead-like click.
Arrow contacts never use metal-light or the generic hit fallback; if their
dedicated assets are missing, those contacts remain silent.
These remain designed Foley effects, not recordings of real arrows hitting
armor or people. No new downloads. Preparation happens once at unlock in
the existing worker. Each accepted impact still consumes one voice and the
shared hit cooldown. Bullets and melee retain their existing contact banks.
The sound preview starts with bow, soft arrow contact, armored arrow contact.
