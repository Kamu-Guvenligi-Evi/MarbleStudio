Production changes:
- crown-battle-timing.js: large live armies request 20 updates per playback
  second; simulation dt is 0.05/0.10/0.20 at 1x/2x/4x. Previously 4x
  requested four times as many full-army updates. Keep 9ms RAF work budget,
  bounded backlog, pending tick completion and responsive pause/commands.
- crown-battle.js: sliced crowd updates accept up to 0.20s. Synchronous
  replay/export and small armies retain their previous timestep limits.
  This is a temporal precision tradeoff: accelerated combat is not claimed
  to produce identical outcomes to the old 0.05s stepping. Swept arrow and
  charge contact, melee wind-up and finite state are regression tested.
- crown-battle-renderer.js: deferred atlas work cannot repeatedly rebuild
  an unchanged crowd tick and restart GPU movement interpolation. Missing
  poses retry on the next completed tick; paused preparation still works.
  Slow completed ticks can interpolate up to 2s instead of freezing after
  500ms. This smooths presentation; it does not increase simulation speed.
- crown-battle-crowd-deployment.js: expanded classic armies >=2,500 start
  in spaced ranks rather than overlapping strips. Cavalry wings and flank
  preset retained; manual deployment, small and historical battles retained.
- crown-battle-npc-routing.js: route history expiry accounts for 0.2s ticks
  and floating-point drift so continuous 4x walking retains its detour state.
- crown-battle.js: blocked troops obey their retarget timer; accelerated
  retry remains possible without searching on every tick. Large autonomous
  armies score 24 target candidates instead of 96; held units retain 96.
  Reuse candidate distance and use sqrt for bounded-coordinate distances.
  Target sampling changes AI choices; no claim of identical battle outcomes.
  Physical projectile/charge contacts remain separate exhaustive queries.
- Tried live Float64 coordinates in SpatialGrid: parity passed, no reliable
  speed benefit; fully reverted that experiment.
- Tried 24-unit physical grid cells: actual-app speed did not improve;
  reverted both the grid and contact-query changes to the 40-unit grid.
