Material contact audio — 2026-10-15

Projectile impacts (arrow, bolt and bullet) now select a short metal contact
for armored targets (existing armor >= 0.14 heuristic) and a soft contact for
other targets. Pikes striking unarmored targets also use the soft bank.
Projectile visuals use armor-hit/body-hit accordingly, without melee sparks.
Mounted archers' sidearm hits can clang against armor; their arrows retain
light projectile weight rather than inheriting cavalry impact weight.
Slow, high-damage infantry contacts use a modestly lower playback pitch.
This is game sound design, not historically measured material acoustics.

BattleAudio loads two separate contact banks through the existing worker,
trims once to 0.22/0.25 seconds, applies conservative gains, and shares the
existing hit cooldown/token budget. One source per accepted hit; eight total
voices remain the limit. No extra runtime filter or queued sounds. Missing
contact clips fall back to the existing hit bank. Projectile contacts do not
activate the metallic melee crowd bed.
