Adaptive battle openings — 2026-10-16

Problem: Milan's front cavalry used eight fixed files and its reserve six.
Increasing the army added depth without frontage, producing the long marching
columns shown in the user's screenshot. Several other doctrines used the same
fixed-grid helper. This helper has been removed from civilization layouts.

Implementation:
- historical-civilization-tactics.js uses corpsBuilder/formationLayout for
  scalable cavalry wings, reserves, infantry fronts and missile sections.
  Reserve depth and intervals derive from occupied soldier extents.
- historical-formation-policy.js selects existing player formations from
  equipment, enemy composition, flank and missile pressure. It contains no
  civilization whitelist. Shared generic and auxiliary deployments use it too.
- historical-french-deployment.js separates foot, missiles, gendarmes and
  coutiliers, with independent wing lanes. Mounted ordonnance archers are
  represented by the catalogue's foot archers in battle, not by a new dismount
  mechanic. The preset scales the 1445 lance's combatant proportions.
- Specialized Ottoman, Ming and Landsknecht cores keep their equipment-based
  geometry. Supporting corps can use the shared formation policy.
- Seljuk preparation now measures health loss across the whole army as well
  as the mounted trap group. Milan exposed a case where auxiliary troops died
  while the untouched cavalry waited for another ten seconds. Both team
  positions are covered by a natural-battle regression in the horse-archer test.

Verification scope:
- historical-adaptive-openings.mjs: every catalogue civilization at capacity
  48/240/960 on both sides; soldier-body clearance and commander assignment;
  no long narrow combat corps. French/Milan variants additionally reach 2400
  capacity against infantry, pikes, cavalry and missiles. Generic leaders are
  checked with identical mixed equipment in four directions and four plans.
- The centre-support regression now uses a fixed direct cavalry assault.
  The adaptive French commander attacks the outer wings, so demanding a
  centre-support flag in that matchup no longer exercises the intended case.
  The direct-assault test still requires dispatch before 10% centre health
  loss, at least 75% centre survival at 20 seconds, and half the dispatched
  soldiers attacking within 15 seconds. Its browser counterpart checks the
  same 20-second centre-survival and dispatch conditions.
- The battle-matrix runner accepts BATTLE_FILTER='Milano|Fransa' to test both
  against every catalogue civilization in both team positions. Zero matches
  now fail rather than reporting an empty success.
