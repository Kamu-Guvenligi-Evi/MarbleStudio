Physics consistency - 2026-10-14
Removed time-varying phase offsets and closing-height scaling: both changed airborne acceleration and could reverse motion without a floor impact. Each ball now follows exact constant-gravity flights, with a fixed per-position launch period for each of the four wave patterns. Opening remains a synchronized drop from rest, landing at 0.825 seconds. The final launch is reduced at floor contact to land at the requested end time. No airborne flattening.
Sphere centers are offset by their radius above the floor so resting balls no longer intersect it. Height changes restart the simulation instead of teleporting airborne balls. Preview clock no longer discards elapsed time above 50 ms; pause/visibility resets still exclude inactive time.

Shaped release correction - 2026-10-14

Individual first-impact times follow sqrt(2*h/g), and elastic rebounds return each ball to its own initial height without adding energy at the floor. The final shortened hop remains a dissipative floor contact for the existing end-of-video landing.
