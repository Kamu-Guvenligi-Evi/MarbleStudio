Historical interpretation:
The Ottoman preset represents a fifteenth-century interpretation, not a universal arrangement for every Ottoman battle. It has no artillery/firearm component. A crescent or feigned retreat is not prescribed for every opponent. Formation dimensions, unit ratios and dispatch timing are game parameters, not documented historical measurements.
HRE: pike block with protected short weapons and flank missiles. Ming: equipment-based teams with shields, wolf brushes, spears and short weapons in separate positions. Both are scaled game interpretations.
